fitPaisa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

fitPaisa ("we", "our") is a walk-to-earn fitness rewards app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your name (from Google sign-in), email address, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Physical activity data: daily step counts read from your device's hardware step sensor, used solely to calculate FitCoin rewards. We do not collect GPS location, routes, or maps.
  • Reward data: coin balances, redemption requests, and payout account details you provide (account holder name and account number for Easypaisa/JazzCash/bank), stored encrypted.
  • Device data: app version and a random install identifier, used for fraud prevention.

2. How we use it

  • To operate the app: count steps, credit FitCoins, and process redemptions.
  • To prevent fraud: detect fake or manipulated step activity.
  • To contact you about your redemptions if needed.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising profiles.

3. Data sharing

Your data is stored with our infrastructure provider (Supabase, hosted in the EU/US) which processes it only on our instructions. Payout details are shared with the payment provider you choose (Easypaisa/JazzCash/your bank) solely to send your redemption.

4. Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app: Profile → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and removes your steps, coins, and payout details. Financial records required by law may be retained in anonymized form.

5. Children

fitPaisa is for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Security

Coin balances are calculated and stored on our secure servers — never on your phone — so they cannot be tampered with. Payout account numbers are stored encrypted. Access to user data is limited to the founder for redemption processing.

7. Your rights

You may request a copy of your data or ask us to correct it by contacting us below. You may withdraw consent for activity tracking at any time by denying the Activity Recognition permission in your device settings (step counting and rewards will stop).

8. Changes

We will post any policy changes here and update the date above.
